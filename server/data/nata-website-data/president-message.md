# President's Message — NATA 2026 overview

Prof. Abhay V. Purohit, President, Council of Architecture.

The National Aptitude Test in Architecture (NATA) has steadily evolved into a reliable and transparent national-level assessment for aspiring architects. The successful conduct of NATA 2025 marked a significant advancement, particularly with the introduction of a more adaptive and responsive examination framework. Building on this experience, the Council of Architecture is pleased to announce the conduct of NATA 2026.

Architecture is a discipline that balances creative expression with social responsibility. As cities expand and the built environment faces increasingly complex challenges, architects are required to respond with sensitivity, innovation, and technical competence. Architectural education and entry into the profession must remain dynamic and forward-looking.

The role of the architect is one of trust and accountability, including sustainability, safety, inclusivity, and heritage conservation.

## Adaptive NATA and what it measures

NATA represents the first formal milestone for students aspiring to pursue architecture as a profession. The adaptive structure introduced in NATA 2025 enhanced the examination's ability to assess spatial reasoning, analytical thinking, visual perception, and problem-solving in a balanced way. NATA 2026 continues this adaptive approach.

## Viksit Bharat and closing message

India's vision of Viksit Bharat 2047 envisages robust infrastructure and sustainable urbanization; architects shape built environments that are efficient, resilient, and rooted in local context.
