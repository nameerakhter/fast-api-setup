## NATA 2026 Student Support System

The NATA 2026 Student Helpline operates through the Helpdesk Ticketing System: <https://help.nata-app.org/>

Students should read knowledge base articles before opening a ticket. If unanswered, register on the NATA Helpdesk and create a ticket. Tickets are typically responded to within **1 working day**. If not answered in time, candidates may call the helpdesk phone numbers below. Telephonic support is for overdue tickets or absolute emergencies only.

### How to register for NATA Helpdesk

1. Open <https://help.nata-app.org/>
2. Click **Sign Up** (top right), enter Name, Email, and CAPTCHA
3. Use the invitation email to set a password
4. After login, click **CREATE TICKET** for your query

### Important guidelines for raising a ticket

- Read Knowledgebase articles first
- Always mention your **NATA Application Number** (if registered for NATA 2025/2026)
- Attach scans or screenshots when needed
- Tickets answered in **1 working day**; phone only if overdue or emergency
